This experiment is about the publishing system itself. The aim is to make small technical
experiments visible without turning the site into a raw transcript archive.

The working rule is simple: private sessions can be messy, but public notes should be
short, reviewed, and useful. Each note should leave a trace of the mission, what changed,
what was learned, and what the next small test will be.
